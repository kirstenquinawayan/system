02 · Scope

Scope and Limitations

Included

  • Vehicle-based services and prices
  • Appointment availability and booking-capacity checks
  • Staff booking and payment management
  • Manual GCash/Maya transfer reference submission; no automatic gateway or debit-card processing
  • Date-filtered reports, CSV export, and PDF documents

Not included

  • Payment status updates after the customer closes the confirmation page (requires a public HTTPS webhook)
  • Automatic email or SMS notifications
  • Automatic confirmation of in-shop cash received
  • Multi-branch or inventory management
Scope is limited to the implemented local system03 / 09

06 · Implemented Features

What the System Demonstrates

  • Server-side validation of contact details, weekday/weekend appointment hours, and hourly intervals from opening.
  • Appointment-slot capacity checks, including concurrent booking protection and cancelled-slot release.
  • Booking confirmation PDF and automatic paid-booking queue; services move from In Progress to Completed automatically.
  • Manual GCash/Maya transfer reference submission with automatic paid status, and in-shop cash payment.
  • Booking-date and appointment-date reports with CSV export and print view.
Payment: the PHP 250 reservation fee is part of the total. Submitting a transfer reference marks the booking as paid without confirming the actual funds. Staff should reconcile transfers in the merchant account. No automatic gateway, QR, or debit-card processing is configured.
